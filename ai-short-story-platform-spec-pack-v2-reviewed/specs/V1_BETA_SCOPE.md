# V1 Beta Scope

Beta begins only after Alpha release gates are stable.

## Candidate Beta expansions

- Official 15k–30k story support after long-story evals pass.
- Second officially optimized language.
- Additional optimized genres.
- PDF/DOCX knowledge ingestion.
- Stronger hybrid retrieval and reranking if baseline retrieval evals justify it.
- Fast and High Quality modes based on measured cost/quality trade-offs.
- Better story-level QA and diagnostics.
- Workspace reusable knowledge collections.
- More writer-level AI actions.

## Still deferred beyond Beta

- Web Research → V1.3.
- reference-derived Style Analyzer → V1.2 unless prioritization changes.
- automatic dependency graph/downstream repair → V1.5.
- audio generation → V2.x.
- collaboration/CRDT → V3.x.
